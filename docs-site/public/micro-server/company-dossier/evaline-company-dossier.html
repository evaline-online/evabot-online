EVALINE (EvaLine) — Company Dossier

Research note (2026-09-07). The consumer brand EvaLine / EVALINE / EVA-line is operated by the Ukrainian legal entity ТОВ «ЕВА-ЛАЙН» (EVA-LINE LLC), ЄДРПОУ 40484497, registered in Chornomorsk, Odesa oblast. Brand-name spelling on the website (“EvaLine”, “EVALINE”, “EVA-line”) differs from the official registered name «ЕВА-ЛАЙН» — this is a naming discrepancy between brand and legal entity, not a separate company. All registry facts below were cross-checked against ≥2 independent public-registry aggregators where possible.

⚠️ Beware of brand-name collisions: evalinekiev.com.ua (a Prom.ua seller, Kyiv-based, phone +380 67 156-14-67 — different phone/email from the manufacturer), eva-line24.ru (Evaline, Krasnoyarsk, Russia), and other shops use similar names but are not the legal entity described here.

5. Legal History (courts, enforcements, inspections)

5.1 Court decisions — 18 documents in the court register [1]

The dominant case is an administrative tax dispute:

Case № 420/39858/24 (8 documents) — ТОВ «ЕВА-ЛАЙН» vs Головне управління ДПС в Одеській області [7]:

  • Subject: recognition as unlawful and cancellation of tax notice-decision № 54397/15-32-07-07-23 dated 12.12.2024 imposing пеня (penalty/late charges) 99 398,89 ₴ (incl. 89 357,59 ₴ under contract № 21/12/2023 of 21.12.2023) for late settlement in foreign-economic activity — late receipt of imported goods worth USD 18 214,40 (40 calendar days late, 14.07.2024–22.08.2024), under Art. 13 of Law № 2473-VIII “Currency” and NBU Board Resolution № 18 of 24.02.2022.
  • Outcome:
    • 09.04.2025 — Odesa District Administrative Court (judge Іванов Е.А.): claim refused.
    • 01.07.2025 — Fifth Administrative Court of Appeal (judges Семенюк, Федусик, Шляхтицький): appeal dismissed, first-instance ruling upheld (document № 128579139) [7].
    • 23.07.2025 — Supreme Court (Cassation Administrative Court, judge Гімон М.М.): refused to open cassation proceedings → the tax assessment became final (lost by the company) [7].
    • 25.07.2025 — Supreme Court ordered refund of the 6 056 ₴ court fee paid for the (dismissed) cassation filing [7].
  • Other categories present in the register: 1 civil, 1 commercial (господарське) among the 18 documents [1]. Details of the remaining ~10 administrative and other documents are in the register link [1]; contents were not individually reviewed (out of scope of this pass).

7.2 Production facts (self-reported by the company — treat as marketing claims)

  • Self-described as “the first Ukrainian manufacturer of EVA (ethylene-vinyl-acetate) polymer products”, full production cycle from sheets to finished goods [6], [10].
  • Claims >500,000 EVA sheets produced for car-mat manufacturers in 4 years (EN homepage, undated snapshot) [6].
  • Claims 15% recycling rate of output and export growth goals (EN about page) [10].
  • Product lines: puzzle mats, tatami/sport mats, car-mat sheets, shoe soles/insoles, foamiran, animal-husbandry mats «Бурёнка»/«Бурьонка», cosplay/craft sheets [6].
  • MinEconomy farm-support program: company news (06.02.2022 and 29.04.2024) states EVA-line mats «Бурёнка» are eligible for the state 25% farmers’ compensation program (participated via Мінекономіки program) [6]. This is self-reported; the program itself is real state policy — company’s listing UNVERIFIED against a Ministry source.
  • UNIC membership: from 2020 to 2021 the company was an official member of the All-Ukrainian Network of Integrity and Compliance (UNIC) [6]. Independently confirmed by UNIC’s own Facebook post “UNIC welcomes new member EVA-Line LLC” dated 25.03.2020 [10] — so verified membership start; end (2021) is company-stated.
  • Private label / contract manufacturing services offered [6].
  • Foreign footprint: EU office & warehouse in Bratislava, Slovakia (Obchodna 37, 81106) on the 2025 contacts page [6]. No Slovak registry entry verified → company registration in Slovakia UNVERIFIED (likely a sales office).
  • Social channels: Facebook “EVA line — перший в Україні виробник продукції з EVA, Chornomorsk” (2,293 likes, 2024) [12]; Instagram @eva_line_ua; YouTube channel [6].
