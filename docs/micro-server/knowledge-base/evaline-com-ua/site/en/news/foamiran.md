## **What is EVA Foam?**

These are sheets of foamed ethylene vinyl acetate EVA, which in appearance and touch strongly resemble suede. Not without reason they have a second name "plastic suede" due to their very smooth and velvety surface. For the manufacture of foam is used EVA material, which is easy to work with. When heated, the material becomes plastic, stretches well, is able to acquire various configurations. When cooling down, foamine retains the given shape, so it is in demand in needlework and other spheres of activity.  


### **Material characteristics**

  * The density of foam is 100 kg/m3.
  * The thickness of sheets of material varies from 2 millimeters to 35 millimeters in increments of 0.1 mm.
  * The level of thermal conductivity is 0.35 W/(m-K).


## **Basic advantages of EVA foam**

  * Unique texture and decorativeness. Products made of EVA foam from our production are colorful and bright, pleasant to the touch.
  * Ecological purity and safety. The material does not emit any harmful compounds into the environment, it is completely safe for humans and pets.
  * Variety of colors. A huge range of colors and shades makes it possible to choose the material to solve almost any problem.
  * Affordable price. Foam is relatively inexpensive, so it is suitable for both beginners and experienced masters, is simply ideal for children's creativity.
  * Simplicity of processing. To work with EVA Foam does not require expensive tools - you only need a heat gun. But at the first stages and without it you can do without it, using different types of adhesives for connection.
  * Plasticity, the ability to hold a given shape. Thanks to these advantages, you can make almost any products. Everything is limited only by the imagination of the master.


## **Areas of application of the material**

  * Manufacture of artificial flowers (floristry). One of the main spheres of application of foam. The material allows you to make bright and colorful flowers for different tasks - orchids, lilies, hibiscus, roses, combined compositions.
  * Wedding accessories. Make brooches, boutonnieres, hairpins, headbands and much more.
  * Interior decoration. With its help you can make unique design compositions that transform residential and commercial premises, make them cozy and original
  * Scrapbooking technique. Foam decorate frames, albums, colorful panels, gifts and so on. The variety of colors and plasticity allow you to create unique design compositions that can not be made from other materials.


When working with foam, it is necessary to correctly select the thickness. To create floral compositions and scrapbooking use a material thickness of 2 millimeters. Thicker foam used for making theatrical masks, crowns, special costumes, rostovyh figures, frames for photos and paintings.
