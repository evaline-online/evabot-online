# Dovetail Mount

10.03.2014

Fastening "dovetail" on the edges of tatami allow to collect the coating of any area and configuration, and a good connection eliminates cracks between the mats.

Thermal and good cushioning properties allow the use of EvaLine coatings on concrete, tiled and other types of floors that will protect athletes from injury. Floor covering EvaLine resistant to moisture, easy to clean and wash.

Mat Mats with dovetail mount are designed for flooring in gyms, fitness halls, gaming, children's rooms, training grounds.

They are very popular among mats on the floor, because they are very elastic and soft, which makes it possible to smooth the pain sensation when falling.
