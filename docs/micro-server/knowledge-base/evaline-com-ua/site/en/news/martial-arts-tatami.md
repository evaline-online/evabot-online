# Martial arts tatami

10.10.2023

Practicing martial arts poses a certain health risk associated with falls and impacts on the floor. Therefore, the halls where martial arts classes are held are always equipped with a soft surface. Heavy and not very soft mats are often used for this purpose. They are replaced by tatami, which is considered the best choice for practicing various types of martial arts.

Tatami or, as it is also called, budo mat is a type of soft floor covering. Its homeland is considered to be Japan, where it used to be used to cover the floor in residential buildings. Now tatami has become an integral component in gyms. This soft flooring can be seen in martial arts classes and gymnastics or acrobatics training. Aikido, judo, kickboxing, kung fu, jiu-jitsu, taekwondo, sambo and other types of fighting become safer thanks to this equipment.

## Why is tatami more comfortable and safer than other gym flooring?

This Japanese invention has many advantages that provide the best conditions for practicing martial arts:

  * Both sides have a non-slip texture. Special notches on the mats provide a reliable grip on the feet of athletes.
  * have low thermal conductivity and create a "warm floor" effect;
  * have high wear resistance;
  * Excellent cushioning helps to soften impacts during falls;
  * reliable dovetail fastening eliminates the formation of gaps between the mats;
  * easy to assemble and disassemble;
  * made of environmentally friendly hypoallergenic EVA material;
  * quick and easy to clean;
  * do not absorb moisture;
  * no pungent odor.
