# FOAM: A UNIVERSAL MATERIAL FOR THE REALIZATION OF BOLD CREATIVE IDEAS

Production and sale of Foam material 

Bright and original three-dimensional flowers and compositions, applications, elements of costumes for animators and puppets - all this is possible to make with the help of light and plastic sheet material foam. Our company produces and sells foam wholesale. Let's look at its features, performance characteristics and applications.

Foam, or as it is often called, plastic suede, EVA foam, foam - is a finely porous foam material, which is light and elastic, has a wide range of colors, easy to cut and heat treatment "remembers" the shape given. For the manufacture of foam is used eco-friendly polymer EVA, which is hypoallergenic and harmless to human health.

### Easy processing

Foam can be easily cut, colored, joined with glue and heat gun.

### Plasticity

The material is able to hold a given shape, which is suitable for volumetric compositions. They look original and realistic.

## Application of Foam

Thanks to its technical characteristics, foam is widely used by needlewomen in various spheres: 

  * in the manufacture of artificial flowers of various sizes
  * decorating postcards, frames and albums, paintings and panels
  * wedding accessories
  * making growth dolls and costumes for animators.


## Characteristics of Foam

  * Density: 100 kg/m3.
  * Sheet thickness varies from 2 to 35 millimeters in 1 mm increments.
  * The level of thermal conductivity - 0.35 W/(m-K).
