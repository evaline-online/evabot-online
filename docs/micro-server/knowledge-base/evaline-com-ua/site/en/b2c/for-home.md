# EVA Home Puzzle mats

from the manufacturer EvaLine

EVA Line PARQUET soft floor has an original design suitable for both children’s rooms, balconies and rooms that have a strict or contemporary interior.

![](https://eva-line.com/images/dom-sceplenie.jpg)

### Special joint

The dovetail joint ensures easy and simple assembly of a flooring of any area. This type of joint makes it possible to assemble any area and avoid slots in the joints with the full mobility of this coating.

### Environmental friendliness

PARQUET soft floor is light, environmentally friendly, and hypoallergenic.

![](https://eva-line.com/images/dom-ecology.jpg)

A high degree of thermal insulation makes it always warm.
