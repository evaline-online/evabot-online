# EVA DOOR MAT

Comfortable, durable and practical solution for the home, flat or office

A doormat is of utmost importance in any home or office. It needs to hold all the dirt and dust from the street while still looking stylish. EVA doormats are perfect for this task. With them your hallway will always be cosy and clean.

![](https://eva-line.com/images/kovrik-design.jpg)

### Functionality and design

Thanks to their unique honeycomb structure, EVA floor mats look very original and the main thing is that they do not allow rubbish to spread around the room as they keep moisture, dirt and dust inside them.

### Safety

EVA material is environmentally friendly and absolutely safe. It does not cause allergic reactions and does not emit odors. Thanks to these features, such mats can be safely used in a home with small children or animals.

### Resistant to deformation

EVA mats are not afraid of physical impact, creases and deformations. Even if you accidentally bend or squeeze the mat, it will quickly regain its original shape. This is very important, especially in heavy-duty use.

### Huge range of sizes and colors

EVA doormats are produced in the size and color you need. You can order such a mat in almost any shade, which will allow you to choose a variant that will harmoniously fit into the interior of the entrance hall.

### EVA doormat - the best solution for home and office

Every owner of an apartment, house or office space knows perfectly well how difficult it is to keep the entrance hall clean. And if in summer the dust on the floor is not so striking, in autumn and winter wet dirty shoes constantly leave marks. Now you don't have to mop the floor all the time, and the EVA door mat will help you with this.

### EVA mat for entrance halls: features and advantages

Home mats made of innovative EVA material will become not only a useful accessory, but also a real decoration of any home or office. Thanks to the unique structure of the material, such a mat will help to maintain perfect cleanliness in the hallway, regardless of traffic, season and weather.

These mats have a special cellular structure in the shape of a "diamond" or "honeycomb" with a depth of 7-8 mm, they perfectly collect and retain dirt, dust, moisture, rubbish and do not allow them to spread around the room. Moreover, EVA mats are waterproof, so they are ideal for clearing snow from shoes.
