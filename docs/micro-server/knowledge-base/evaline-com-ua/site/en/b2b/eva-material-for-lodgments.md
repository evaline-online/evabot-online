# EVA-MATERIAL FOR LODGMENTS

Sheets of foamed polymer in bulk for the manufacture of lodgments

A lodgment is a packaging element in the form of a tab or stand for fixing any product. It allows you to securely pack the goods for safe transportation and storage. EVA material from EVA-line can be used to produce bright and high-quality lodgments for various applications.

![](https://eva-line.com/images/logement1.png)

#### EVA lodgments are used for packaging:

  * Measuring devices
  * Tools
  * Weapons
  * Glass products
  * Souvenirs


#### Protective packaging made of modern EVA polymer has the following advantages:

  * Environmentally friendly hypoallergenic material
  * Good cushioning properties
  * Light weight
  * Elasticity and strength
  * Water resistance
  * Neutrality to chemicals
  * Wide color palette


![](https://eva-line.com/images/logement2.png)

![](https://eva-line.com/images/logement3.png)

#### Characteristics of EVA sheets

  * Material: foamed ethylene vinyl acetate / EVA (EVA)
  * Sheet thickness from 2 mm (1 mm pitch
  * Color: black, gray, orange, red, yellow, blue, green, khaki and others.


## Order EVA for lodgments from EVA-line

EVA-line is a Ukrainian manufacturer of EVA sheet material. You can order smooth and embossed sheets of various characteristics (thickness, density, hardness, and color) from us. The material is easy to process, so you can work with a laser or milling machine when creating lodgments.
