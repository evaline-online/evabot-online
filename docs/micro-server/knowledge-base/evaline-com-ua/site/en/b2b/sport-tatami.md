# Sports floor covering

When choosing the right material for martial arts class, stretching, yoga or functional training, Evaline Tatami Mats are the best solution. This innovative flooring meets all European standards of quality, and is produced using the latest developments and cutting-edge technologies. Evaline Tatami mats are hypoallergenic, elastic, easy to wash, do not absorb moisture, have high thermal-insulating properties, do not contain halogen, resistant to temperature drop as well as have long service period. The mats are moisture-resistant, easy to clean and wash.

![](https://eva-line.com/images/sport-kreplenie.jpg)

### Special joint

The dovetail joint on the edges of the tatami makes it possible to assemble a covering of any area and configuration, and a good connection excludes slots between mats.

### Thermal insulation

Thermal and good shock-absorbing properties make it possible to use Evaline Tatami mats on concrete, tiled and other types of floors, protecting the athletes from potential injuries.

![](https://eva-line.com/images/2.1 sport_pokryt.jpg)

![](https://eva-line.com/images/sport-antiskolzenie.jpg)

### Anti Slide

The embossed texture on the mat surfaces increases the adhesion of the coating to feet of athletes. Special Anti-Slip embossment enhances the efficiency of adhesion with the mats and prevents slipping even more.
