# MANUFACTURER OF EVA SHEETS AND FINISHED PRODUCTS

We work and deliver EVA foam across Europe

EVA sheets are used for:

  * production of auto carpets;
  * costumes and other handmade accessories (these include cosplay costumes, craft, various stage costumes, and decorations);
  * floor and wall soundproofing;
  * material for martial arts class, stretching, yoga or functional training;
  * for shoes production;
  * mats for cows etc.


![](https://eva-line.com/images/1.1b2b.jpg)

### Wide color range

All positions have a wide range of colors that will allow you to perfectly choose the material.

### The advantages of this material

  * Material is easy (vesicular structure), has a high-tensile strength to weight and flexibility
  * Thermoplastic – retains the attached shape when heated, without disintegration of its internal structure
  * High wear resistance
  * Easily treated by any mechanical method: cutting, sand-papering, pattern cutting, etc.
  * Hypoallergenic


![](https://eva-line.com/images/1.2b2b.jpg)

### Specifications

  * Material: ethylene vinyl acetate / EVA
  * Sheet thickness from 2 mm (0.1 mm spacing)
  * Color: orange, red, black, yellow, blue, green, khaki and others.
