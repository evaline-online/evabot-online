# EVA material seal:

An innovative technology for various applications EvaLine

EVA seals are becoming increasingly popular due to their versatility and high efficiency in a variety of industries, from industry to households.

## Areas of application of EVA seals:

  * **Automotive Industry:**

They are used to seal car doors, glass panels, preventing noise and water from entering the car interior.

  * **Medical Industry:**

Used in medical devices to seal and prevent moisture and bacteria from entering.

  * **Footwear Industry:**

Used to create insoles, soles and other shoe parts because of their lightness and comfort.

  * **Construction and Renovation:**

Seals for sealing windows, doors, bathtubs to prevent water and air infiltration.

## The advantages of using EVA seals:

![](https://eva-line.com/images/news/211/chernaja8-1000x1000.jpg)

###  Flexibility and high elasticity:

EVA material easily adapts to different shapes and sizes, providing high airtightness.

### Waterproof:

The material's ability to prevent water penetration makes it ideal for applications where water resistance is important.
